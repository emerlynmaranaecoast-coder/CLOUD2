The ECOAST Innovation Campus

An inspiring, high-performance physical and digital environment designed for engineers, coders, and makers.

RESEARCH FACILITY 01

Quantum & Supercomputing Cluster

Our premier computing facility features an on-premise supercluster powered by 32 NVIDIA H100 Tensor Core GPUs with 1.2 PetaFLOPS FP64 compute capacity. Undergrads and faculty researchers run molecular dynamics, train custom LLMs, and conduct quantum gate simulations directly via internal JupyterHub nodes.

NVIDIA H100 80GB SXM5 InfiniBand 100Gbps Low Latency 2 Petabytes NVMe Storage Slurm Workload Manager
RESEARCH FACILITY 02

Autonomous Robotics & Mechatronics Arena

An indoor testing space outfitted with sub-millimeter Vicon optical motion capture cameras, automated obstacle courses, and autonomous drone landing pads. Students assemble robotic arms, program ROS2 navigation stacks, and test self-driving miniature vehicles.

Universal Robots UR5e 6-DoF Arms Vicon Optical MoCap Tracking Velodyne 3D LiDAR Sensors ROS2 Humble & Gazebo Sim
COMMUNITY FACILITY 03

24/7 Hacker Lounge & Venture MakerSpace

Innovation doesn't keep 9-to-5 hours. Our 24/7 Hacker Lounge gives student startup founders and hackathon teams continuous access to ultra-fast fiber internet, 3D printing farms, SMD soldering stations, and acoustic ideation pods.

10Gbps Symmetrical Internet Bambu Lab 3D Printing Farm Weller Soldering & Rework Stations Sleep Pods & Café Bar
