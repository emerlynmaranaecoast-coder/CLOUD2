Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about admissions, accreditation, facilities, and student life at ECOAST.

What does ECOAST stand for, and is it recognized by CHED and ABET? +
ECOAST stands for the Engineering Computing Academy of Science and Technology. All our undergraduate degree programs are recognized by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and curricula are structured in direct alignment with international ABET (Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology) criteria for computing and engineering.
Can non-STEM Senior High School graduates apply to ECOAST? +
Yes! We welcome students from ABM, HUMSS, GAS, and TVL-ICT tracks. Non-STEM qualifiers undergo our free 4-week Pre-Calculus & Algorithmic Foundations Summer Bridging Program prior to the start of the first semester, ensuring everyone succeeds in engineering.
How does the 100% Presidential Scholarship work? +
High school graduates With Highest Honors (or those scoring in the top 1st percentile of the E-CAT entrance exam) are awarded the Presidential Scholarship, which covers 100% of both tuition and laboratory fees throughout the 4-year degree, subject to maintaining a GWA of 1.75 or higher.
What computer/laptop specifications are required for incoming students? +
Any modern laptop with an Intel Core i5/AMD Ryzen 5 processor, 16GB RAM, and 512GB SSD is sufficient for coursework. For heavy deep learning, CAD, and FPGA simulation, students are provided unlimited cloud compute instances on our on-campus NVIDIA H100 supercomputing cluster.
Are campus dormitories and high-speed internet available? +
Yes, the ECOAST Tech Village offers secure on-campus residences with 10Gbps symmetrical fiber internet, 24/7 hacker study pods, ergonomic workstations, and recreational facilities.
