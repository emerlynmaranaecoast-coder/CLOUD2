🎓 Senior High STEM Strand

  • ✓ Certified True Copy of Form 137 / 138 (Report Card)
  • ✓ Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • ✓ PSA Authenticated Birth Certificate
  • ✓ 2x2 ID Photos with white background (2 copies)
  • ✓ Certificate of Honors / General Weighted Average (if applying for Presidential or Dean's Scholarship)

🔄 Non-STEM / TVL-ICT Graduates

  • ✓ Form 137 / 138 (General Weighted Average of 85% or higher)
  • ✓ Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • ✓ PSA Authenticated Birth Certificate
  • ✓ Enrollment in the Free 4-Week Pre-Calculus & Python Bridging Camp
  • ✓ Endorsement letter from High School Principal / STEM Advisor

🏛️ College Transferees

  • ✓ Official Transcript of Records (TOR) or Certificate of Grades
  • ✓ Honorable Dismissal / Transfer Credential Certificate
  • ✓ Course Descriptions for subject crediting evaluation
  • ✓ PSA Authenticated Birth Certificate
  • ✓ Minimum GWA of 2.25 with no failing grades in math or science
