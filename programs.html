BACHELOR OF SCIENCE

Computer Science — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

4-Year Program • 168 Total Credit Units • 600-Hour Industry Practicum
Apply

The BSCS Artificial Intelligence track is engineered for students who want to build the future of autonomous intelligence. Students master foundational mathematics (linear algebra, multivariate calculus, discrete math), data structures, and state-of-the-art deep learning architectures including Transformers, Diffusion Models, and Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning. All students receive guaranteed GPU compute hours on our NVIDIA H100 supercomputing cluster.

Core Subject Modules

Deep Neural Networks Natural Language Processing & LLMs Computer Vision & NeRFs Reinforcement Learning Distributed AI Training AI Model Safety & Alignment

Embedded Professional Certifications

Students are sponsored to take and pass: NVIDIA Certified Associate — Generative AI and AWS Certified Machine Learning Specialty.

Career Pathways & Salaries

  • AI / Machine Learning Engineer (₱75k - ₱140k/mo)
  • Computer Vision Specialist
  • LLM Solutions Architect
  • Autonomous Systems Developer
  • Data Science Research Associate

BACHELOR OF SCIENCE

Computer Engineering — Robotics & Cyber-Physical Systems

4-Year Program • 172 Total Credit Units • PRC Board-Eligible
Apply

Fusing hardware engineering with embedded computing, BS CpE at ECOAST trains engineers to build autonomous mobile robots, smart drones, industrial automation lines, and medical robotic manipulators. Students work in our Autonomous Mechatronics Arena using ROS2, ARM Cortex microcontrollers, FPGA silicon chips, and digital signal processors.

Core Subject Modules

Robot Operating System 2 (ROS2) Microprocessor Architecture (RISC-V) FPGA & Verilog HDL Autonomous SLAM Navigation Embedded RTOS (FreeRTOS) Industrial Mechatronics

Lab & Hardware Facility Access

24/7 access to the Mechatronics Arena, Universal Robots collaborative arm workstations, 3D additive manufacturing lab, and high-frequency oscilloscopes.

Career Pathways & Salaries

  • Robotics Systems Engineer (₱70k - ₱135k/mo)
  • Embedded Firmware Developer
  • IoT Hardware Architect
  • FPGA Design Engineer
  • Industrial Automation Specialist

Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics

4-Year Program • 165 Total Credit Units • Live Cyber Range Practicum
Apply

Defend critical infrastructure and uncover advanced cyber intrusions. Students train inside ECOAST's dedicated Security Operations Center (SOC) running offensive Red Team simulations, defensive Blue Team monitoring, malware reverse engineering, packet dissection, and digital forensics following global ISO/NIST standards.

Embedded Professional Certifications

Direct preparation for: CompTIA Security+, Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH), and Cisco CyberOps Associate.

Career Pathways & Salaries

  • SOC Security Analyst Tier 2/3 (₱75k - ₱150k/mo)
  • Penetration Tester / Red Teamer
  • Incident Response Investigator
  • Cyber Threat Hunter
  • Information Security Auditor
